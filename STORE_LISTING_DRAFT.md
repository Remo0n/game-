**Working name:** Bento Blocks

**Subtitle:** A cozy slice-and-fit puzzle

**Short description:** Slice colorful food blocks and pack a perfect bento, one cozy puzzle at a time.

**Description:**

Make room for a little puzzle break.

Cut colorful blocks into pieces, then arrange them to fill your bento tray.
Start with a simple slice and discover new shapes, clever layouts, and different
ways to play as your kitchen grows.

- Slice along the seams and find a place for every piece.
- Play a fresh daily bento and build your daily streak.
- Try special challenges, including one-cut, exact-fit, and timed puzzles.
- Earn stars and tools, and unlock illustrated food, trays, and backgrounds.
- Take your time with free undo, helpful hints, soft sounds, and gentle motion.

Progress is saved on your device. There is no account or cloud sync.
